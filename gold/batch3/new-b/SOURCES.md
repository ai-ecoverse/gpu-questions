Selection used the batch-3 copy of `hf_extract.py` with `--root data/hf3b --more-formats` and `--seen-datasets gold/handlabeled.jsonl gold/batch2/handlabeled.jsonl`. Additional local extractors handled the Ranga message-list export, `ppak10` conversations, monodox turns, and Betterwright parquet. The parquet source used eight pinned shards (141 MB) and only the final assistant answer from each run. Candidate text was scrubbed for secrets, home paths, IPs, phone numbers, and emails, then reviewed individually. The final sample keeps all 113 valid addressed-question turns and 75 varied negatives (60 hard); 96 other reviewed negative blocks are retained in `reviewed_unused.txt`. No dataset contributes more than 150 labels, and no session contributes more than three across datasets. The `hf-coding-tools-traces` publications reuse underlying session IDs, so they were globally capped and should not be treated as independent corpora.

**Held-out protection.** `introvoyz041/my-personal-codex-data` mirrors held-out `REXX-NEW/my-personal-codex-data`. Its 33 earlier labels, 37 queue rows, 290 candidates, downloaded files, and manifest entry were removed. Its 290 candidates contained 75 turns from 26 held-out session IDs. Every other extracted candidate source and every final record was checked against `data/batch3/test_sessions.txt` using full session values, basename, and `#` suffix; there were no other overlaps. New sources were checked before labeling. Obvious reuploads such as `focustiki/sft-coding-agent-traces` (derived from synthtraces) were not used.

Privacy drops included c0007 (full-name request), c0170 (private chat excerpts), c0186 (personal repository namespace), c0188 (write-token request), c0312 (physical address), c0410 (credential discussion), and an unlabeled Ranga item with a database connection string. Their sensitive values are absent from annotations and output. Other claimed/downloaded sources produced no usable final turns, had ambiguous agent provenance, lacked an eligible license, or were dominated by benchmark status reports. This limited the final count below the roughly 1,500-turn target.
